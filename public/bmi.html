What BMI is not. It cannot tell muscle from fat, it says nothing about where weight sits on your body, and it was devised to describe populations rather than individuals. A muscular athlete and someone carrying visceral fat can share a number.

Treat it as one line in a longer conversation — alongside your labs, your history and how you actually feel. That conversation is what a consultation is for.
