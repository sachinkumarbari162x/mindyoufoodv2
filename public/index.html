The building blocks

Macros & micros

Six things every plan has to get right. Not rules to fear — levers to use, in the amounts your body actually needs.

Macros

  • Protein

    0.8–1.2 g per kg body weight. Repairs tissue and keeps you full.

  • Fibre

    25–30 g daily. Feeds the gut microbiome.

  • Carbohydrates

    45–60% of daily energy. Your body's preferred fuel.

  • Fats

    20–35% of daily energy. Essential for hormones.

Micros

  • Vitamins

    Trace amounts, µg to mg. Essential for immunity and repair.

  • Minerals

    Trace amounts, µg to mg. Critical for energy and bone strength.

General nutrition guidance, not medical advice. Your numbers depend on your labs, medication, activity and history — that is exactly what a consultation is for.

How we work together

A method built on accountability

  1. Step 01

    Listen & assess

    We start with a proper conversation, not a questionnaire. Your medical history, any recent labs, the medication you take, how you sleep, how you actually eat on a bad week — and what you have already tried that did not hold.

    Most plans fail because they were built on a guess about one of those. Nothing gets designed until the picture is complete.

  2. Step 02

    Personalize

    Then the plan is built around the food you already eat — your kitchen, your culture, your budget, and the time you genuinely have on a weekday evening. It works with your hormones and whatever we are managing rather than around them.

    If something in it does not fit your life, it is the wrong plan. We change the plan; you do not have to change your life to fit it.

  3. Step 03

    Stay accountable

    We meet regularly, and you lead those sessions. You tell me what worked, what you quietly dropped, and where the week fell apart — none of which is a failure. It is the information the next adjustment is built from.

    My job is to keep the plan honest and the pace realistic. The ownership stays yours; what you never have to do is any of it alone.

  4. Step 04

    Sustain

    As things settle we take the scaffolding away — fewer rules, fewer check-ins, more of it running on habit rather than on effort.

    The aim is a way of eating you could keep for years without thinking about it, not a protocol you are relieved to finish. By the end you should be able to handle a holiday, a hard month, or a change in your health without needing me to rebuild it.

From the journal

Myths, minded

Myth

Carbs make you gain weight.

Quantity, quality, and context decide outcomes — not a single macronutrient. The right carbs support hormones and energy.

Myth

Skipping meals speeds up fat loss.

It often slows metabolism and triggers later overeating. Consistent, balanced meals work far better.

Myth

Detox teas & cleanses reset your body.

Your liver and kidneys already do that. Real results come from sustainable food habits, not a 7-day fix.
